# Sources: JEE Main 2025 (ExamGOAL copies)

NTA does not publish the 2025 papers openly (they are shown only inside the candidate login). On 2026-10-25 the owner decided to use ExamGOAL's retyped papers and ExamGOAL's answer keys for 2025, 2022 and 2021. These are NOT NTA's own documents: questions were retyped by ExamGOAL, NTA question IDs are not present, and the answer key is ExamGOAL's. In a check on three papers we also have from NTA (2026 2 Apr S1, 2024 27 Jan S1, 2023 6 Apr S1), every ExamGOAL numerical answer matched NTA's final key; MCQ keys could not be compared that way. Chapters (topics) come from each question's ExamSIDE page title (examside_titles.json).
