# Sources: JEE Main 2022 (ExamGOAL copies)

NTA does not publish the 2022 papers openly. On 2026-10-25 the owner decided to use ExamGOAL's retyped papers and ExamGOAL's answer keys for 2025, 2022 and 2021. These are NOT NTA's own documents: questions were retyped by ExamGOAL, NTA question IDs are not present, and the answer key is ExamGOAL's (on the NTA papers we can compare, ExamGOAL's key sometimes differs from NTA's final key; see key_compare.csv in those shifts). 2022 papers have 90 questions (30 per subject, as in the ExamGOAL copies). Chapters (topics) come from each question's ExamSIDE page title (examside_titles.json).
