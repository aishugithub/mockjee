    General Instructions

    Please read the instructions carefully

    1. Total duration of this test is .
    2. The countdown timer in the top right corner of the screen shows the time remaining. When it reaches zero, the test is submitted automatically.
    3. The Question Palette on the right shows the status of each question using one of the following symbols:
      1 You have not visited the question yet.
      2 You have not answered the question.
      3 You have answered the question.
      4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
      5 You have answered the question and marked it for review. This answer will be considered for evaluation.
    4. Click a question number in the palette to go to that question directly. Going to another question this way does NOT save the answer to the current question.
    5. Click SAVE & NEXT to save your answer and move to the next question.
    6. Click SAVE & MARK FOR REVIEW to save your answer, mark it for review and move on. Click MARK FOR REVIEW & NEXT to mark the question for review without saving a new answer.
    7. To deselect a chosen answer, click CLEAR.
    8. For numerical-value questions, enter the answer with the on-screen keypad.
    9. Sections in this test are shown on the top bar. Click a section name to view its questions.
